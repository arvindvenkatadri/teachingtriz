## {{< iconify fluent-mdl2 decision-solid >}} Conclusions

- Flow is a `Zone of Play` between the rising `Floor of Boredom` and the approaching `Ceiling of Anxiety / Challenge` ( my "definition"!)
- You need to use the Boredom to push away the Ceiling to stay in Play
- To do that, you may need to:
  - Break Rules: Change how you experience / perceive things
  - Understand your Assumptions: ( More on this later)
  - Use and Create Surprises: Spot the Problem lurking in your Boredom
  - Grow More Skills to handle higher Complexity


## {{< iconify mingcute thought-line >}} Wait, But Why?

- Flow is a great state to be in when you are working
- It enables you to experience your work, your problem solving, in a way that is unique and hyper productive
- And...
- If you build / design some thing, wouldn't you want flow for the people who use that thing?
- Flow is your gift to your users / customers / clients / audience !!
- To think of others having Rich Lives, or a need for Complexity / Vividness / Challenge and Joy in their lives may sound *delusional*, but there is a word for it: [sonder](https://www.dictionary.com/browse/sonder)
- And thinking of Impossible Things is a good idea, people, as we will see!
- And so, it is both a beginning, and an end in Design!!
